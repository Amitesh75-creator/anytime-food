Andhra curry and biryani pastes that do the hard part. You just bring the chicken.

All the flavour of slow cooking, none of the grinding.

What's in the jar stays simple.

Three things to do.

  1. 1

  2. 2

    Add your chicken

    Coat it well and let the paste get to work.
